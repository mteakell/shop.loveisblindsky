Why Western Kentucky homeowners call us first

Love Is Blinds is owner-operated. The person who measures your windows quotes them on the spot, and our own crew installs. No subcontractors, no showroom trip, no guessing at sizes. We bring the full sample case to your home so you compare colors and fabrics in your own light.

Built for lake country

Kentucky Lake humidity is hard on cheap window treatments. We spec moisture-resistant faux wood and composite where it matters, solar fabrics that cut water glare without losing the view, and motorized shades that protect a weekend place while you are away.

Every product, custom built to your openings

Plantation shutters, roller shades, faux wood blinds, banded shades, outdoor patio shades, and full motorization. Everything is ordered to the measured opening, which is why it fits square and operates smoothly for years.
