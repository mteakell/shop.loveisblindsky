Evening sun, handled outside the porch

An interior shade only works after heat is already in. A patio shade mounts at the porch opening and intercepts sun and glare before they reach you. Mesh fabrics keep the breeze and the view while cutting the beat-down.

Fixed, roll-up, or motorized

Fixed panels for a permanent opening, manual roll-ups for flexibility, and motorized shades that retract at the push of a button or on a wind schedule. We spec mounting for your posts, beams, or masonry.

Common questions.

Do patio shades block the view?
No. The mesh is see-through from inside looking out, even at tighter weaves. You lose glare and heat, not the lake.
How do they handle wind?
Cable or track guides keep motorized and roll-up shades steady, and retracting in high wind is the honest answer for storms. We spec guides to your porch.
Will they keep bugs out?
Tighter meshes meaningfully reduce insects on a covered porch, though they are shade products first, not sealed screens.
Can they be automated?
Yes. Motorized patio shades run from a remote or schedule, which is perfect for west-facing porches that only need cover in the evening.
