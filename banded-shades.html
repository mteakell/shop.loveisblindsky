How the bands actually work

A banded shade, sometimes called a zebra shade, is one continuous fabric loop of alternating sheer and solid stripes. Align the sheers and you see out. Offset them and the solids overlap for privacy. One shade, two modes, no second treatment needed.

Where they shine

Living rooms and offices that want daytime view with evening privacy, street-facing windows, and modern interiors where the horizontal banding reads as a design feature. For true blackout sleeping rooms we will honestly point you to a different shade.

Common questions.

Can you see through banded shades at night?
In sheer mode with interior lights on, yes, silhouettes show. Offset to solid mode for evening privacy. For total darkness, a blackout roller is the better tool and we will say so.
Are banded shades good for bedrooms?
For light sleepers, no, the sheer bands always pass some light. For guest rooms and spaces where style matters more than blackout, they work well.
Do they motorize?
Yes, banded shades motorize cleanly, and band alignment by remote is genuinely convenient.
How are they cleaned?
Light dusting and spot cleaning. The continuous loop means no slats to wipe individually.
